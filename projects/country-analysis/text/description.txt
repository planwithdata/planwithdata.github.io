International Development - A comparison of 5 countries' historical growth

Project Overview:
Examined the development experiences of individual nations (Japan, Canada, India, US, South Africa) in order to devise a set of policy insights relevant to city and regional planning.

Research Questions Addressed:
• How have the trajectories of population, real GDP, real GDP per capita, and urbanization evolved over the past 60 years?
• Explanation of these trends, with reference to deeper library research, statistical analysis, and data gathering
• Forecasting population, real GDP, real GDP per capita, and urbanization levels for 2025, 2030, and 2035
• Geographic analysis of where growth and change impacts will be experienced within the nations
• Comparison of recent socioeconomic outcomes relevant to the selected nations
• Stylized facts and general recommendations for policymakers

Countries Analyzed:
1. Japan - Post-war reconstruction and economic miracle
2. Canada - Resource-based economy and urbanization
3. India - Emerging economy and rapid urbanization
4. United States - Mature economy and suburban development
5. South Africa - Post-apartheid development challenges

Methodology:
- Historical data analysis using Stata
- Spatial analysis using ArcGIS Pro
- Report design and layout using InDesign
- Statistical modeling and forecasting
- Comparative policy analysis

Key Deliverables:
- Comprehensive analytical report
- Statistical models and forecasts
- Policy recommendations
- Comparative analysis framework

Tools Used:
- Stata for statistical analysis
- ArcGIS Pro for spatial analysis and mapping
- InDesign for report design and layout
- Various data sources and databases
