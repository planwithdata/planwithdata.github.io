Heat Sensitivity Index - Richmond, VA

Project Overview:
This project assesses the vulnerability of different census tracts in Richmond region to heat stress. This index incorporates a variety of socio-economic, health, and demographic variables to provide a comprehensive measure of heat sensitivity.

Methodology:
- Data collection from census and health departments
- Statistical analysis using Python
- GIS mapping and spatial analysis
- Index calculation and validation

Key Findings:
Population in the Richmond urban center is more vulnerable to heat stress with sub regions like south Richmond, west Henrico, and Charles City being highlighted more than others.

Technologies Used:
- Python for data analysis
- ArcGIS for spatial analysis
- Statistical modeling techniques
- Web development for visualization

Impact:
This index can be used to identify vulnerable areas and guide resource allocation and policy interventions to mitigate the effects of heat stress.
