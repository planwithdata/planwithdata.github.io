EV Sales in Delhi, IND - 2024

Project Overview:
Comprehensive analysis diving into the latest trends and insights on electric vehicle adoption in India. This project explores market dynamics, policy impacts, and future projections for the electric vehicle sector in Delhi, providing valuable insights for stakeholders in the automotive and policy sectors.

Analysis Components:
- Electric vehicle sales trends and patterns in Delhi
- Market dynamics and adoption rates
- Policy impact assessment on EV growth
- Infrastructure development analysis
- Future market projections and forecasting
- Comparative analysis with other regions

Key Deliverables:
- Detailed analytical report with comprehensive findings
- Interactive EV Dashboard for real-time insights
- Delhi EV Infrastructure Map for spatial analysis
- Policy recommendations and market insights

Technologies and Tools:
- Python for data analysis and processing
- Natural Language Processing for market sentiment analysis
- Artificial Intelligence for predictive modeling
- JavaScript for interactive web applications
- HTML/CSS for web development and presentation
- GIS technologies for spatial analysis

Data Sources:
ev.delhi.gov.in and Manufacturer or commercial EV sales websites 

Key Findings:
The rapid growth of the EV market in Delhi is commendable, driven by increasing sales and a general shift towards cleaner, greener mobility. However, the charging station infrastructure is still evolving, with certain gaps in distribution and cost disparities across the city. Addressing these challenges will be key to sustaining the momentum in EV adoption. Investments in expanding charging networks, ensuring affordability, and improving station capacity in underdeveloped areas will be essential as Delhi strives to meet its electric mobility goals.
